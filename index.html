Common Central AC Questions in Poway, CA

Get immediate, transparent answers to frequent central air conditioning repair questions in inland San Diego County.

When an air conditioner blows room-temperature air, the indoor blower is running but the outdoor condenser is failing to absorb and dissipate heat. The most common culprits are a blown dual-run capacitor preventing the compressor from starting, low R-410A refrigerant charge from a joint leak, a frozen evaporator coil choking off heat transfer, or a tripped outdoor electrical disconnect. Dispatched technicians perform pressure and electrical tests to isolate the exact cause.

Capacitors fail far more frequently than compressors, especially during hot Poway summers. A failing capacitor will often present with a distinct humming sound from the outdoor unit while the fan blades fail to turn, or a visibly swollen or domed metal top. Technicians test microfarads (uF) with a digital multimeter. A capacitor replacement is a fast, low-cost repair, whereas a compressor replacement is a major mechanical repair requiring EPA refrigerant recovery.

During humid or hot periods, your evaporator coil pulls gallons of condensation out of the air. If the primary PVC condensate drain line clogs with algae, slime, or dust, water fills the drain pan. If the emergency secondary drain pan is cracked or lacks a functioning float safety switch, water spills over directly into attic insulation and drywall. Technicians clear the clog with nitrogen blowouts and install float shutoff switches to prevent water damage.
