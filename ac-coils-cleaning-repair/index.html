The Thermal Engines of Heat Transfer

Your central air conditioner contains two separate coil assemblies: the indoor evaporator coil (typically mounted atop your furnace or in an attic air handler) and the outdoor condenser coil (wrapping around the exterior unit). The evaporator coil absorbs heat from indoor air, while the condenser coil discharges that heat into the outdoor air. If either coil is coated with dirt or dust, thermal conductivity plummets, forcing your compressor to work dramatically harder while producing noticeably less cooling.
