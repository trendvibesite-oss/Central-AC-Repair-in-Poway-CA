Comprehensive Diagnostic & Repair Process

When you request service through our referral dispatch platform, an independent licensed technician conducts an exacting diagnostic evaluation rather than relying on guesswork. Here is what to expect during your on-site service appointment:

  1. Electrical & Voltage Assessment: Technicians inspect incoming line voltage, dual-run capacitor capacitance (µF ratings), contactor point pitting, and electrical disconnect fuses to ensure safe operation.
  2. Thermodynamic & Refrigerant Pressure Testing: Digital manifold gauges measure suction and liquid line pressures, verifying proper superheat and subcooling levels to rule out thermal expansion valve (TXV) restrictions or low Freon charge.
  3. Airflow & Static Pressure Diagnostics: Static pressure probes assess airflow across the indoor evaporator coil and blower assembly, identifying restricted ductwork or dirty filters that threaten compressor longevity.
  4. Upfront Written Estimate: You receive an itemized, transparent quote detailing necessary replacement parts and labor before any mechanical repair commences.
  5. Precision Repair & Operational Verification: After installing factory OEM components, the technician runs a complete cooling cycle, measuring supply and return temperature split (typically 16°F to 20°F) to confirm optimal cooling performance.
